- **Log and deck widths swapped, board shifted to match:** the log is now 310 px and the deck 260 px (`lgw`, `ldw` in `PLAY_LAYOUT`). The board is centred in the window and does not react to the side panels, so a new knob, `lbx` (Board X shift, in the lab's Table section), pads one side of the page to move the table, hand, hero figures and energy bar together (they all sit relative to the table); it is baked at 50. The played-card popup is fixed to the window, so its `lcx` moved by the same 50 (-252 to -202). The gaps between the board and each panel are what they were. A new log knob, `lgtg`, sets the space above each turn bar independently of the space between actions (`lggp`); the first turn bar has none.
- **The log is redesigned to read at a glance:** log lines are now structured (`LogLine` in `log-line.ts`, built by `describeEvents`) instead of strings. Each action is one block with a stripe in its side's colour, so no line repeats "Player 1"; cards are coloured by whose they are; results sit under their action as coloured chips (red `-6`, green `+3`, blue armor, gold buffs, purple debuffs, grey deaths) with dim notes at the right ("1 left", "28 left", "dead", "1 absorbed", "now 4/4"). A turn header is a bar with the player's name, the only real break. Sans-serif 13px by default. Icons are emoji in one table (`LOG_ICON` in `describe-events.ts`), to swap for in-theme icons later. Attacks and deaths say whose minion it is through the colour, and a hero's health left is tracked through armor, heals and earlier hits. The board lab's Log/deck tab is now six accordions (Layout, Log text, Log action blocks, Log result chips, Log turn breaks, Test log) with knobs for the stripe width, block radius and padding, icon size and visibility, card name weight and colour, chip colours per kind (damage, heal, armor, death, buff, debuff), chip width and radius, result indent, note size and colour, turn bar colours and uppercase. All are `lg...` params that `npm run board` bakes into `PLAY_LAYOUT`; the monospace knob `lgft` now defaults to off.
- **The log names what caused damage, and the board lab can restyle it:** a trigger or On Death effect now adds a `TriggerFired` event (`settle.ts`), shown as "Nuclear Submarine triggers" before its damage lines instead of a bare "takes 8". Turn headers and indented effect lines get their own classes. The board lab's Log/deck tab is now four accordions: Layout, Log text (size, line height, font, padding, radius, indent, colours for background, text, card names and effect lines), Log turn breaks (spacing, line thickness and style, colour, header size, weight and colour) and Test log (`llpv`: load an empty, typical or every-kind-of-line sample; not baked). The style knobs are `lg...` params; `npm run board` bakes them into `PLAY_LAYOUT` like the rest.
