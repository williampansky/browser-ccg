# Changelog

What changed, newest first. Written from the git history; `git log` has the detail.

## 2026-11-20

- **Choose dialog shows the real cards:** the dialog for "choose one" cards, Surveying Drone and the Cleric's Resurrect is now one component (`ChooseModal`), and each option is drawn as the actual card in its frame, with a caption under it. An option with no card of its own (and a Drone slot with an empty deck) shows a blank NONE-rarity card (steel frame, no gem, placeholder art, no cost number) named for the choice. Board lab: new "Choose dialog" section on the Hand tab (`lcmw` card size, `lcmg` gap, `lcmd` backdrop darkness, `lcmt` title size, `lcmhs` / `lcmhl` / `lcmglow` hovered card scale, lift and glow, `lcmc` caption on/off) and a "Hold it open" preview (`lcmpv`, a sample choose-one card). The knobs bake with `npm run board` like the others (`lcmpv` is a preview switch, so it is skipped).
- **Art lab with an entourage card:** `/art-lab?id=CORE_020a` now works. Every entry point upper-cased the id, turning the entourage letter into `CORE_020A`, which matched nothing (the data, Airtable's `{id}` lookup, the master in `art-inbox/` and the `020a-CARD.jpg` files all use the lower-case letter). One `canonicalCardId` now fixes the spelling (set and number in capitals, the letter in lower case) in the page, the card, master and render routes, `npm run art` and the master lookup.
- **Card overlay: parent of an entourage card, and click to swap:** viewing an entourage card now shows the card(s) that make or offer it, under "Made or offered by", where its own tokens would go. The related cards (a card's tokens, or an entourage card's parents) are buttons: click one to view it in the same overlay, click back to return. Clicking elsewhere still closes it.
- **Collection: Entourage follows the set filter:** with Entourage ticked, Core shows only Core entourage cards and Prime only Prime ones (they used to ignore the set filter). A token's set comes from its id (`CORE_...` / `PRIME_...`).
- **Log card view on click:** Menu > Settings > "Card view from the log" (Right-click / Click; kept in this browser, `ccg.logCardTrigger.v1`) chooses whether a card or skill name in the log opens the big card view on a right-click (default) or a single left-click.
- **Stampede icon fades:** the Stampede badge on a board minion is only useful the turn it came in, so from the next turn on it fades and shrinks away (about 1.4 s) and stays gone.
- **Druid balance:** Forsaken Druid's Spirit Animal now costs 3 (was 2) with cooldown 3 (was 2) in `data/abilities.json`, from the hero sim (Druid 85% overall before, 67% after; the other heroes each gained 1 to 6 points). **Copy both numbers into Airtable** (ABILITIES, Druid skill 1), or the next `npm run airtable` puts the old ones back.
- **Cleric Resurrect is a pick:** skill 2 now offers two minions drawn at random from the last five that died on your side, each with Guard or with Bubble (one dialog, up to four buttons; `engine/heroes/resurrect-offer.ts`), and a 15% chance one of them is the strongest of the enemy's last five dead (it leaves their graveyard). With nothing dead on your side the skill can't be used. The offer comes from the turn and both graveyards, so nothing extra is stored. New for skills in general: a skill can list `options` and the `HeroSkill` action takes a `choice`, like a "choose one" card. In the hero sim Sage went from 42% to 58% overall with the cost left at 6. **Copy the new skill text into Airtable** (ABILITIES, Cleric skill 2: `%RESURRECT% a recently dead friendly minion of your choice with %BULWARK% or %BUBBLE%`), or the next `npm run airtable` puts the old text back.

## 2026-11-19

- **Spell Damage raises hero skills:** your minions' Spell Damage now adds to each damage hit of a hero skill (Zeus' three, Exile's first, both Warrior damage skills), so a Spell Damage +1 minion turns Zeus' Lightning Strike from 1 into 2 while it is on the board. Heals, armor, summons and buffs are unchanged; skills still are not spells for "when you cast a spell" effects. `RULES.md` Appendix D updated (it used to say skills ignore Spell Damage).
- **Log: hero skills and right-click:** hero skill names in the log now have their hover text (they were missing from the name lookup, so the tooltip was empty), and right-clicking any card or skill name in the log opens the collection's big card view, like right-clicking a minion. A skill's card (cast popup and big view) now takes its art from the hero's skill files in one place (`CardFace`).
- **Hero skill cast popup:** it no longer forces Mythic. The frame comes from a `rarity` column on the Airtable ABILITIES table (`COMMON`, `SUPERIOR`, `REMARKABLE`, `MYTHIC`, `FREE`; blank = `FREE`, steel with no gem; **add the single-select column by hand, then `npm run airtable`**: the pull now carries it into `data/abilities.json`). The art is `public/images/heroes/<HERO>/skill-<n>-card.jpg` (any shape, covers the card window), falling back to the round `skill-<n>.jpg`, then the placeholder. The deck panel heading now shows the deck's name too.
- **Deck rows show the full card on hover:** resting the pointer on a deck entry for `hoverDelay` ms (deck lab, Row section, `d_hoverDelay`, 0-3000, default 600; bakes with `npm run deck`) pops up the full framed card, replacing the plain text tooltip. It opens on the side with room (a row in the left half of the window opens it to the right, one in the right half to the left). Once one is showing, moving to another row shows its card at once (250 ms grace after leaving); only the first one drifts in, subtly, from the deck's side (off with reduced motion). Also fixed: the `-DECK` strips ignored the row's size (the `background` shorthand only fitted the last of the stacked images), so they now cover the row.

## 2026-11-18

- **Deck lab and `-DECK` strips:** `/deck-lab` (dev only) edits how the deck panel's rows look, and the rows now show each card's `-DECK` strip (600 x 75; falls back to the `-CARD` art, then the placeholder) behind the cost gem, name and count, under a shade that lets the name read. Sections: the strip (zoom, where it is centred, opacity, brightness, saturation, blur), the shade (colour, strength left and right, how fast it changes, how much is left under the pointer), the row (height, gap, radius, accent bar, padding, colours), the cost gem, name and count, and a used-up card (opacity, greyness). Every value is a `d_<field>` URL param (`DeckTune` in `components/play-board/deck-row-tune.ts`); `/play` reads the same params over `DECK_DEFAULTS`, and `npm run deck -- "<lab url>"` bakes a lab URL into those defaults. The row is one shared component (`DeckRowView`), so the lab and `/play` cannot drift apart. `d_showArt=0` brings back the old plain rows.
- **Connected sidebar (log + deck):** a player can switch the log and deck from two panels to one connected sidebar in the `/play` menu (Settings > "Log and deck": Separate / Connected; kept in this browser, `ccg.sidebar.v1`). Connected puts them flush in one column with a drag bar between them: drag it to give the log or the deck more room (the deck scrolls when it has less than its rows need), double-click to reset; the split is remembered (`ccg.sideSplit.v1`). It sits on the side the saved layout stacks on (the right unless `lpan` says left). Board lab: the Log and deck arrangement has "Connected sidebar on the left / right"; when it is chosen the two width sliders, the log's share of height and the deck offsets give way to one sidebar width (`lsbw`), one total height (`lsbh`, px; unset = fill the window), the drag bar thickness (`lsbd`) and one X / Y offset for the whole column. The drag bar measures against the column's real height. `lpan` bakes with `npm run board`, so a saved layout can start connected. The lab's Arrangement select now writes every choice into the URL, including "Log left, deck right" (`lpan=default`): before, that choice only removed `lpan` and the lab fell back to the saved arrangement, so it could never show while a stacked or connected one was saved.
- **Hover pace (opt-in):** board lab `lhsd` (ms, 0 = off): the hovered card changes at most once per `lhsd` ms, one card at a time, and keeps catching up toward the pointer, so a quick sweep or a far jump fans across the cards instead of snapping. A hover that has been still for that long moves at once, so a slow sweep is not held back. A click still acts on the card under the pointer.
- **Fan tilt and drop per hand size:** board lab Hands section, "Fan tilt and drop by number of cards": `lhrt1` .. `lhrt10`, `lhdp1` .. `lhdp10` and `lhol1` .. `lhol10` (card overlap, the spacing) each replace the main tilt (`lhrt`), drop (`lhdp`) or overlap (`lhol`) while the hand holds exactly that many cards (the hand carries `data-count`). An untouched count starts from the main tilt, so nothing changes until one is set. The section also has a "Cards in my hand now" box to step through sizes.
- **Hand hover slots:** the pointer is now tested against one column per card (the "hover slot": at the card's resting place, it never lifts, grows or parts), not against the card, which ignores the pointer. A lifted, enlarged card no longer blocks the cards beside it, so sweeping along a tight hand swaps the hovered card straight away. The hovered card's slot reaches up to its lifted height; the hover look lets go 70 ms after the pointer leaves, so gaps do not flicker. Click, double-click, drag and the drag ghost all work through the slot; a selected card is still clicked on its own face. Board lab: "Hover slot width" (`lhsw`, % of the card spacing, 0 = the old behaviour), "Hovered slot: extra width each side" (`lhsh`), the outermost slots reaching out to the card's outer edge (`lhse`, 1 = to the edge) and further (`lhsx` px), and a "Show the hover slots" preview (`lhspv`).
- **Hand arc:** a new "Fan curve" knob in the board lab (`lhdc`, 0 = the straight V of the drop, 1 = a round arc): the middle cards stay high and the drop concentrates toward the ends. Default 0, so the saved hand looks the same until it is set.
- **Middle strip, separate placement:** the End turn button and the status text each have their own "sits at the left / middle / right" (`lmdba`, `lmdta`) and their own move right / move down nudges (`lmdbdx`, `lmdbdy`, `lmdtdx`, `lmdtdy`). Middle puts the item on the strip's exact centre (out of the flow, so the strip can get shorter: use its minimum height). They bake with `npm run board`.
- **Hero ring takes a hit:** when health or armor drops the ring shakes (`hitShake` px, `hitTilt` deg, `hitShakeMs`) and the amount floats up from the middle (`popSize`, `popRise`, `popMs`, `popY`, colours `hitColour` / `healColour`; healing shows a green `+n`). Both wait `hitDelay` ms (the attack's strike, 190 by default). All are `r_` fields in the hero lab's ring tab (new "Hit shake and damage number" group, plus Hit 3 / Hit 8 / Heal 3 buttons) and bake with `npm run ring`.
- **Board lab, Hands:** 16 new hand knobs (all CSS variables on `.hand`, so `npm run board` bakes them like the others): fan tilt and drop, card overlap, hover scale / lift / tilt / speed, **parting** (the hovered card's neighbours slide away; off by default), hover halo and flash, settle time and draw-in time. "Hold card number N in its hover look" (`lhpv`) tunes the hover without the pointer, and "Cards in my / their hand" (`hn0` / `hn1`, a setup param like `p0`, also valid on `/play`) deals a hand of 0-10. Overlap changes spacing only: the shrink-to-fit zoom still assumes 20 px.
- **Cast popup for everything:** every card played (minions and weapons too, not only spells and items) and every hero skill (not only ultimates) now shows in the cast popup. A face-down Mystery still does not. The minion's hand-to-board flight is gone (the popup replaces it); `CardFlight.tsx` and `flightFrames` are left in place, unused.
- **Hero ring:** a metal bezel picture behind the disc (`bezel`, % of the disc; `public/images/ui/hero-ring-bezel.png`) and a metal frame around the skill, weapon and Mystery sockets (`frame`, px across; `hero-socket-frame.png`, drawn in a layer behind every socket, pill and gem, with its edge faded so the halo is not cut off). The weapon socket and its frame are hidden when no weapon is equipped. A Mystery socket (`mysteryAngle`) shows while a Mystery is set: the card art for its owner, a "?" for the opponent, with a hover tip. A hovered skill now lifts above its neighbours so its tooltip is not covered.
- **Hero lab (ring tab):** the controls are a left sidebar of collapsible groups beside a scrolling preview, so a slider can be dragged without scrolling the page; a Mystery preview dropdown.
