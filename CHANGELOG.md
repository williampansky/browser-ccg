# Changelog

What changed, newest first. Written from the git history; `git log` has the detail.

## 2026-11-15

- **Stampede lunge fix:** a minion with Stampede that attacked right after being played lunged while it was still invisible or falling in (card flight + 520 ms landing), so the lunge was never seen. The lunge now waits until the minion has landed (`landedAt` in `PlayBoard.tsx`); the attack itself still resolves at once. The direction is read when you attack (the target may be dead by the time it lunges), and an attacker that dies in the exchange lunges as its death ghost (it used to lunge on a minion that had already been removed, so no lunge showed).
- **Kill preview ("doomed") look:** a minion the aimed action would kill now also has its portrait darkened (brightness 0.45, saturation 1.5, white behind it) as well as the skull over it (`doomedPortrait` in `board-minion.module.scss`; the skull class is renamed `doomedIcon`). The minion lab gallery has a "Will die" sample showing it. `MinionFace.tsx` was also reformatted to the repo's Prettier config.

## 2026-11-14

- **Card sounds:** playing a card (a minion or a spell) and drawing a card now use recorded sounds (Kenney's Casino Audio, CC0): 4 `card-place` and 8 `card-slide` variants, picked at random with a little pitch variation. They load when the board opens, follow the Sound switch, and the old synth tone is the fallback if a file is missing. The card sounds do not count toward the 3-sound cap, so a spell that kills a minion keeps its death sound. Credits in the README.
- **New defaults from the lab's `minion-shadows` preset:** `/play` and the lab now start with the `paper-v2` parchment on the play surface (darken 0.1), drop shadows at darkness 0.75 (default, ready, can be aimed at), 0.65 (aimed at) and 0.15 (selected, pushed 27 px down), a card-play sound that is only `card-place-2` at volume 0.35 (pitch variation 0.07), hand hover at volume 0.15 (variation 0.03) with a 290 ms gap, and the held-card loop at 0.65. The layout values are in `PLAY_LAYOUT` (`layout-css.ts`) and the sound ones in `sounds.ts`; the lab sliders show them as the starting values ("none" in the Background image picker turns the background off).
- **Minion shadows (board lab, Board tab > Minion shadows):** the dark drop shadow under a board minion's oval is now a set of variables per state (default, ready to attack, selected / lifted, can be aimed at, aimed at), each with a downward offset, blur and darkness (`--sh-<state>-y|b|a` in `board-minion.module.scss`, knobs `lsh<state>y|b|a`). Defaults keep the old look: default and ready are 3 px / 12 px / 0.55, and selected, aimed and hovered start at 0 (no shadow).
- **Board lab Background section** (Board tab): pick an image from `public/images/backgrounds/` (listed by `/api/backgrounds`; first two are the Croomfolk "Old Paper Cards" parchments), put it on the play surface or behind everything, fill or tile it, and set darken, blur and focus point. The CSS is `backgroundCss` in `layout-css.ts` (`lbg*` params), so it can be adopted into `/play` through `PLAY_LAYOUT` later. Not on `/play` yet.
- **Board lab tabs:** a tab bar at the top of the lab panel (Setup, Board, Hand, Log/deck, Heroes, Sounds), each holding its own accordion sections; the open tab is remembered. Which tab holds which section is `LAB_TABS` in `components/board-lab/lab-sections.tsx`. The URL's sound settings now apply whichever tab is open (`useSoundTuning`).
- **Board lab split into small files** (`components/board-lab/`): `lab-model.ts` (the knob table, one entry per slider), `lab-panels.tsx` (one small component per section), `LabOverlay.tsx` (the floating panel), `PresetsPanel.tsx`, `SoundPanel.tsx`, `LabSliders.tsx`, `lab-sections.tsx`. `pages/board-lab.tsx` is down from 627 to 100 lines. Behaviour is unchanged.
- **Board lab: Sounds section.** For card play, card draw, hand hover and the held-card loop: pick any file under `public/sounds` (listed by `/api/sounds`), set the volume and the pitch variation, set the hover gap, and press Play. Values live in the URL (`lsf_*`, `lsg_*`, `lsj_*`, `lsgap`) and change the sound engine live (`setSoundTuning` in `sounds.ts`); `/play` never sets them.
- **Stop button** next to each Play in the lab's Sounds tab: cuts off any recorded sound still playing, a pending hover sound and the held-card loop. Play on "Held card (loop)" now keeps looping until you press Stop (it used to stop itself after 2 s), so you can hear a long loop's seam.
- **Held-card sound option:** `public/sounds/cards/magic-glow.m4a` ("Magic Glow", CC0, 46 s, 330 KB as AAC) is in the lab's Sounds file picker for any slot; pick it on "Held card (loop)" to try it. The default is still the fireflies loop.
- **Keyword icons:** the row of small keyword icons along the top of a board minion is nudged 0.35 px left by default (minion lab `lkx=-0.35`).
- **Dragged card returns cleanly:** letting go of a card that is not played used to show two copies for a moment (the real hand card popped back while the drag ghost was still flying in and fading). The real card now stays hidden until the ghost has landed (~110 ms), then replaces it in one frame (`whenDone` in `drag-ghost.ts`).
- **Minion tooltip delay and right-click inspect:** the hover tooltip on board minions waits `MINION_TIP_DELAY_MS` (800 ms, set by the author) before showing. Right-clicking a minion opens the collection's big-card view of it (the shared `CardDetailOverlay`, extracted from the collection; Esc or a click closes it). While something is selected, right-click still just cancels the selection.
- **Board lab: Copy config / Paste config** on each accordion section: copies that section's values (the URL's, else `/play`'s saved ones) as `key=value&...`; paste applies only the keys that belong to that section.
- **Portrait scale fix:** the hover scale used to override the selected one while the pointer was still on a selected minion (the hover rule was more specific). Selected now wins.
- **Portrait scale on hover (board lab, Board tab > Minions):** the minion's picture is now its own layer inside the oval (which clips it), so it can scale without scaling the frame. Knobs: scale on hover (`lpsh`), scale when selected (`lpss`), speed (`lpsm`) and the scale's centre from the top (`lpsy`). All default to no change (1x). It is on the board lab and not the minion lab because it is an interaction, not a static look.
- **Hand fan feel:** the hover gap default is now 35 ms (was 290) so every card you cross sounds as you cross it, and the hover sound is trimmed to a 150 ms flick (with a 30 ms fade) so quick hovers stack into a ruffle instead of one long slide. New `Length (ms)` knob in the lab's Sounds tab (`lsm_<slot>`; 0 plays the whole sound).
- **Hover sound fix:** a hover that came inside the gap was dropped for good, so on a sweep a card could be silent even when you rested on it. It now plays when the gap ends if the pointer is still there (a newer hover replaces it; leaving the card cancels it).
- **Hand hover sound:** moving the pointer onto a card in your hand plays `card-slide-5` quietly, with wide pitch variation and a 55 ms minimum gap so a sweep across the hand is a run of ticks. Not while dragging, or while a card is selected.
- **Held-card sound:** a soft looping shimmer ("Magic Fireflies", CC0, 130 KB as AAC) fades in while a hand card is clicked or dragged and fades out when it is let go or played. It follows the Sound switch.
- **Double-click to play:** a quick double-click on a minion in your hand plays it at the far right of your board (a minion that needs aiming, or a choose-one card, stays selected for the normal flow).
- **Kill preview:** while aiming (attack, spell or skill), a skull (placeholder glyph) shows over every minion the action under the pointer would kill, including your own attacker when the counter-hit kills it. It tries the action on a copy of the game with the opponent's Mystery left out, so it never reveals it.
- **Weapon on the hero figure:** the figure shows the equipped weapon's attack and durability as two badges (dashed outline when it only lasts this turn). Temporary look while the design is worked out.
- **Hero balance sim:** `npm run sim:heroes -- heroes|decks <games>` plays hero against hero on the same starter deck (`heroes`), or the starter-deck grid with heroes drawn from the five next to the same grid without (`decks`). First full run (greedy bot): Forsaken Druid 91%, Zeus 60%, Warrior 51%, Exile 46%, Sage 34%, no hero 17%. First tuning (data edits, to copy into Airtable): Spirit Animal cooldown 1 to 2, Revitalize heals 3 (was 2). Druid is still at about 83%, so more is needed.
- **`/play` uses the board lab's "wip" layout** (`PLAY_LAYOUT` in `components/play-board/layout-css.ts`, shared with `/board-lab`, which now matches `/play` with no params; an `l...` URL param overrides one knob). Hero figures are the default (`?herofig=0` for the old bar). Log and deck are 242 px wide.
- **Tooltips stay on screen:** `viewport-nudge.ts` (`nudgeIntoViewport`, `useKeepTipsInViewport`; CSS tooltips carry `data-tip`).
- **Interchangeable interface themes:** the site chrome (page colours, bottom nav, markdown pages, profile) and the `/play` menus (menu button, menu, deck picker, choose-one dialogs) now read `--ui-*` CSS variables, so a whole look can be swapped. Three themes: Classic (the old look, the default), Emberhold and Frostforged (the Emberhold UI kit: 9-slice panels and buttons, toggle art, its menu sounds). Pick one in the `/play` menu, on `/profile`, or with `?ui=<id>` on any page (saved); `/ui-lab` (dev only, not linked) shows all of them side by side. Cards, minions and heroes are untouched. The collection, deck builder, heroes viewer and profile are themed too, and every control on them (and in the `/play` menu) now comes from shared components in `components/ui/` (`Button`, `Select`, `TextInput`, `Checkbox`, `Toggle`, `SegmentedControl`, `Panel`), so a theme or a behaviour change reaches all of them; the mana-cost and card-size buttons are now proper radio groups. **Animated icons** from the Basic Shapes packs (22 crosses, plus signs, dots and a cross-into-checkmark, as 96 px frame sequences, 1.4 MB in all and loaded only when an icon first plays): an `AnimatedIcon` component that takes the text colour and plays forward or back; the checkbox tick uses it. A gallery is on `/ui-lab`. Form rows (`ui-controls`) give every control one height per theme, so Clear filters appearing no longer moves anything. Disabled controls all show the not-allowed cursor (checkboxes, drop-downs and switches used to show a pointer), switches and segmented controls can be disabled, and a search box's clear (X) button is drawn in the theme's colour. Animated icons are no longer stretched (the dots were ovals), are sized by their resting shape so crosses and plus signs match, and draw nothing when off (the Regular Cross left corner marks behind). Toggles now slide (the knob moves with a slight spring and the track fades between off and on; the kit themes use `toggle_track_*` and `toggle_knob_*` pieces cut from the kit's toggle images), and the segmented control's chosen option keeps its whole outline (its left edge was missing) while chips have uniform corners. The drop-down arrow now takes the field's text colour (it is drawn from gradients, not a grey picture). Adding a theme is one entry in `components/ui-theme/ui-themes.ts` plus one block in `styles/themes/_themes.scss`. Themed menus can play the kit's click, toggle and open/close sounds (they follow the existing Sound switch; Classic is silent).
- **Board minion look (tuned in the minion lab):** the attack badge is 44px (was 40) and 1px lower, the health badge 2px lower, the attack numeral moved to (2px, 4.2px) and the health numeral 0.5px left, the keyword icons 4px lower, and the minion's name is no longer shown on the board (it is only visually hidden, so screen readers still get it; `/minion-lab?lcshow=1` shows it). The offsets are CSS variables (`--nudge-x` / `--nudge-y`) with these defaults, so the lab's sliders start from them.
- **`/minion-lab`** (dev only, not linked): the board minion on its own, in a gallery of looks (plain, Guard, Bubble, Concealed, status icons, damaged, buffed, suppressed), with sliders and number boxes for the overall shape (height, aspect, corner radius, border, picture offset, guard shield) and for where the attack and health badges, the name and the keyword icons sit (the keyword icons also have size, gap, ring, corner, glyph and per-keyword colour controls), plus each badge's own numeral size and position. Also a state preview (ready, selected, aimed) and a preview zoom. Sliders start at the real values, and the URL holds every setting.
- A clicked minion now lifts (14 px) as well as growing (1.08x). The growth never actually showed before: the landing animation held its last frame and overrode the selected transform, so it is now `backwards` instead of `both`. A target you aim at no longer scales on hover (ring only; it was meant to be 1.1x but never showed). The board lab can tune the lift, the scale and the target-hover scale, and every slider has a number box for exact values.
- **`/board-lab`** (dev only, not linked): the real `/play` board frozen into a setup you choose, with a draggable, collapsible overlay of layout knobs. Everything is in the URL, so a layout can be shared. No bot and no clock; boards, energy and whose turn it is change live without touching the hands or decks (heroes and hand ids restart it, with a fixed seed so the deck is the same each time). Knobs: minion size and gap, the energy bar (scale, X/Y, vertical or horizontal), section gap, table width, hand sink, both hands' card widths, the clicked-card scale (with a preview), both hero figures' width and X/Y position, and the log / deck panels (swapped, or stacked and scrolling independently). Sliders start at the board's real values. Presets save the URL (all of it, or layout only), with a Flip A/B button. `PlayBoard` takes an optional `lab` prop for this; `/play` does not pass it.

## 2026-11-13

- A card dragged to aim at a target no longer grows to the click's 2x; `.prettierrc` added (the hero skills component is reformatted to it).
- **Weight (second pass):** dead minions fall, spin and fade before the row closes; a minion played from your hand is flown to its slot before it lands; hits, landings and deaths have a heavier thud, played at the moment of impact; the spell popup slams in with a shockwave; "Attack hero" lunges each attacker in turn; tooltips no longer fly off to the far side when you aim an attack (they render into the page body and hide while aiming).
- **Weight (first pass):** new minions drop in, land with a squash and a dust ring; attackers wind up and lunge into their target (minions and weapon swings); the target shakes on contact, not before; damage and heal numbers float up from minions and heroes; a hit of 4+ jolts the table. All of it is skipped under reduced-motion. `components/play-board/motion.ts`.
- **Energy bar:** a tall column of energy orbs beside the board (right edge), with the count above. Dragging or selecting a card pulses the orbs it would spend (red when you cannot afford it).
- **Playtest fixes:** a selected hand card is bigger (2x), hero skill tooltips open over the minions, the Attack number on board minions is centred on its badge, and hero tokens (Spirit Animals, Eagle) get a real tooltip instead of "undefined", and a hovered hand card draws over the hero bar and skills.
- **Hero art:** the "1" / "2" / "AI" circle on the hero bar is now the hero's portrait (still shown when a player has no hero), and the skill buttons are round icons with a cost badge, a cooldown number and the name and text on hover. Art is in `public/images/heroes/<ID>/` (copied from `react-ccg-deprecated`, resized; provenance unchecked like the rest).
- **Bot fix:** the greedy bot no longer sees through your face-down Mystery. It used to simulate its moves on the real state, so it avoided
  any attack the Mystery would punish (it could stop attacking for turns); now it plans without the Mystery and just attacks into it.
- **Heroes:** five heroes (Zeus, Forsaken Druid, Exile, Warrior, Cleric) with three skills each, usable on `/play`: skill buttons above
  your hero bar (click; targeted skills then aim), cooldowns and one-use ultimates, picked in New game (or `?h0=ZEUS&h1=WARRIOR`).
  The AI uses skills too. Readings I chose are in `RULES.md` Appendix D.
- `/collection` is a browser of every playable card (Core + Prime) with search and filters and a card detail window.
- `/profile` is the page for your name and colour (moved out of the `/play` menu).
- The `/play` menu is redesigned: Resume, New game and Concede cards, switch rows for sound, timer and accessibility,
  segmented Opponent and Mode choices, and Home / Deck builder / Cards links (asks before leaving a live game).
- The bottom nav has a Home button and highlights the current page.
- Home, News and Changelog render the repo's `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
