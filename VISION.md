# Vision

## What this is

A browser-only, turn-based collectible card game. It was inspired by the original
Hearthstone (before expansions). It has since moved toward a more structured format
(zones, a fixed number of turns, simultaneous reveals) influenced by Marvel SNAP.
The first attempt at the Hearthstone style proved too hard to build by hand, which is
why the design changed.

Today it is single-player against a bot.

## Background

Passion project, started by hand in 2020 and set aside in 2023. All code up to now was
written by hand, before AI coding tools. The intent is to eventually grow it into a
real multi-user game, possibly with some form of monetization. There is no committed
timeline or definition of "done" yet.

## Priorities

Most important:

1. **Card design and balance.** The cards and their mechanics are the game.
2. **Multiplayer.** Moving from player-vs-bot to player-vs-player and multi-user.
3. **An enjoyable experience.** It should feel good to play.

Secondary:

- Store and collecting
- Visuals
